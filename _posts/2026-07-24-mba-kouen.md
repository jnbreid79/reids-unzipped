Ilot Mba Kouen is another small island inside New Caledonia's lagoon, just a few miles further from Noumea than [Ilot Signal](https://jnbreid79.github.io/reids-unzipped/2026/06/22/signal-zipper-is-back.html). We decided to visit for a weekend whilst we were waiting to lift out in Numbo to fix up an oil leak that developed on our starboard saildrive. Better fix it here in New Caledonia where we have the facilities than risk a bigger problem in the coming months when we will be more remote.

![New Caledonia lagoon map showing Mba Kouen]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/posts/new-caledonia-lagoon-map.webp)

Mba Kouen is another gem! The island itself is tiny so provides no wind protection, however the reef is large so the water is beautifully calm. Amazing for kitesurfing; finally after 2 years I made it out and dusted off the rust, launching from and even managing to land back on the boat thanks to some help from Sophie! Thanks also to Dave on *Kotare* for spotting an opportunity to practice his amazing drone flying skills and capture the footage!

With seagrass on the bottom there are turtles all around the anchorage, and some big ones too. However the real highlight came from seeing a dugong swimming not far behind the boat (yes ok, a fleeting glimpse, but still...).

The children loved the beach, yet another glorious patch of soft white New Caledonian sand, while Sophie and I snorkelled in the shallows. We were enchanted by friendly turtles and some white tip reef sharks, even though it was so shallow in parts we felt we had to breath in to keep above the coral and the fish.
